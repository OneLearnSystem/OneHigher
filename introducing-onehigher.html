INTRODUCING ONEHIGHER · 3 OCTOBER 2026

Your next chapter, connected.

Meet OneHigher, the new further and higher education branch of OneLearn.

OneHigher is a space for the next stage of education: colleges, universities and the people learning and working within them.

Meet the family.

ONE College focuses on college courses, attendance and student support. ONE University brings a distinct direction for programmes, modules and campus life.

Alongside them, OneApply and OnePath are planned services for applications, enrolment, careers and placements.

A clearer identity.

Coral, charcoal and a calmer layout give OneHigher its own character while keeping its connection to OneLearn.

This website introduces the service family. It is not an announcement that every service is ready for use. We’ll share progress and confirm access directly with institutions as development continues.
