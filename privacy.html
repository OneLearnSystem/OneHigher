About this website.

Browsing

This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Pages and images are served by the website host, which may process technical connection information as part of providing the site.

Contacting us

The contact form builds an email draft in your browser. Form entries are not saved to a website database or browser storage. They are sent to the OneLearn team only when you choose to send the email through your email provider.

The team uses the details you send to review and respond to your enquiry. Please send only information relevant to your request and avoid including student records or sensitive personal information. You can contact the team about the information you have supplied using the same enquiry address.

Other services

Linked portals are separate services. Your institution will provide information about how your data is handled within those services.
